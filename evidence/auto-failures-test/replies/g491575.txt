# Tenancy Agreement Summary Rubric

Built from your own gathered inputs: the task, good and bad examples, and what a missed failure would cost.

Score each area from 1 to 5.

| Score | Meaning |
| ---: | --- |
| 1 | Unsafe or unusable |
| 2 | Weak, needs substantial correction |
| 3 | Useful with careful review |
| 4 | Strong with minor corrections |
| 5 | Accurate and ready to use |

## Scoring Sheet

| Area | What to Check | Score |
| --- | --- | ---: |
| Accuracy of key terms | Rent, due date, deposit amount, term length and notice periods match the agreement exactly. Check each against the document, not against the summary's wording. | / 5 |
| Conditions and caveats kept | Conditions survived, for example "increases only with two months' written notice" and not just "rent can increase". | / 5 |
| Stated versus absent | Anything the agreement does not say is reported as missing, not filled in. Example: "The agreement does not name a deposit scheme. Check this before signing." | / 5 |
| Deposit handling | The summary states what the agreement says about the deposit: amount, who holds it, which scheme (if named), and any time limit mentioned. It does not describe protection as in place unless the agreement says so. | / 5 |
| Missing information flagged | The summary lists what the landlord should check or ask about before signing, rather than leaving gaps unmentioned. | / 5 |
| Quoting and sourcing | Key terms are quoted or point to a clause, so the landlord can find them in the agreement. | / 5 |
| Plain language for the reader | Written for a small landlord, not a lawyer. Clear enough to act on without hiding the exact terms. | / 5 |
| Check, not clearance | The summary helps the landlord check the key terms. It does not say the agreement is fine, legal or safe to sign. | / 5 |

## Automatic Failures

Fail the output regardless of its score if it:

- States that the deposit is protected, or held in an approved scheme, when the agreement names no scheme (the failure in your bad example)
- States any other term as present or confirmed when the agreement does not contain it, for example a scheme name, a deposit amount, a time limit or a notice period (the same mistake in a nearby field)

## What to Record

- Total score
- Any automatic failure
- The single most important correction made
- What would need to change in the prompt or process before the next attempt
