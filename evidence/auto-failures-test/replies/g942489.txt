# Supplier Invoice Entry Rubric

Built from your own gathered inputs: the task, good and bad examples, and what a missed failure would cost.

Score each area from 1 to 5.

| Score | Meaning |
| ---: | --- |
| 1 | Unsafe or unusable |
| 2 | Weak, needs substantial correction |
| 3 | Useful with careful review |
| 4 | Strong with minor corrections |
| 5 | Accurate and ready to use |

## Scoring Sheet

| Area | What to Check | Score |
| --- | --- | ---: |
| Total | The entered total matches the printed total digit for digit, including the decimal point and every zero. Check it against the original, not against the AI's own summary. | / 5 |
| Invoice number | The invoice number is copied exactly as printed, with no dropped, added or swapped characters. | / 5 |
| Supplier | The supplier name matches the invoice and the name on your accounts system record, not a similar-looking supplier. | / 5 |
| Due date | The due date is exactly as printed, with the right day, month and year. If only payment terms are shown, the output says so rather than working out a date without telling you. | / 5 |
| Unclear or unreadable values | Anything cut off, blurry or ambiguous (like your page 2 example) is flagged for a person to check against the original, not guessed. | / 5 |
| Missing information | Fields or references that are absent are named, such as the missing purchase order number in your first example. | / 5 |
| Confirmed versus inferred | The output makes clear which values were read directly from the invoice and which were worked out or assumed. | / 5 |
| Left for approval | The entry is prepared for finance to approve. It is not described as approved, paid or final. | / 5 |

Eight areas, under the ten maximum.

## Automatic Failures

Fail the output regardless of its score if it:

- Enters a total that differs from the printed total, such as a dropped, added or changed digit (14,180 entered as 1,418), without flagging it.
- Makes the same kind of misreading in a nearby field, such as a wrong invoice number, due date or supplier, without flagging it.

I have kept this list to the failure you actually had and the same mistake in the fields next to it. I have not added anything nobody has described. If you have seen another outcome that was serious enough to belong here, tell me what happened and I will add it. Otherwise things like a missing PO number or a guessed unclear value are covered in the scored areas above.

## What to Record

- Total score
- Any automatic failure
- The single most important correction made
- What would need to change in the prompt or process before the next attempt

A note before you rely on this. It is a starting point, not a finished tool. Run it against a handful of real invoices first, including a few with large figures, a cut-off scan and a missing PO number, and check that the scores match what a careful person in finance would conclude. Because your costly mistake was a single dropped digit, it is worth including at least one invoice where the total has a thousands separator or a large amount, so you can see whether the Total check catches it. Revise the rubric once real use shows an area is missing, or one that nobody ever scores low.
