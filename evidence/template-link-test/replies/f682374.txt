## Rubric: AI ticket priority (P1 to P4)

You score each ticket the AI prioritised. You score the priority and the reason together, not the priority alone.

### Scored areas (1 to 5 each)

1. **Deadline caught.** Did the AI notice a time limit in the ticket ("pay run is at noon") and raise the priority because of it? This is the area the payroll ticket failed.
2. **Impact caught.** Did it weigh how many people, and which business function, are affected? Finance, payroll and whole teams count for more than one person's desk.
3. **Meaning over wording.** Did it judge what the problem stops from happening, rather than how serious the words sound? "Will not open" sounds minor. "Pay run" is not.
4. **Facts kept apart from guesses.** Does the reason say what the ticket states, and mark anything the AI is assuming?
5. **Missing information flagged.** If the ticket leaves out something that changes the priority (the deadline, how many people, whether there is a workaround), does the AI say so, rather than rating it confidently anyway? (My guess at a useful area.)
6. **Reason is short and checkable.** One line a human can read in seconds and confirm or dispute against the ticket.
7. **No invented urgency, and no invented calm.** It does not add a deadline the ticket never gave. It does not say "no impact" when the ticket says nothing. (My guess.)

Seven areas is enough. Do not add more until real use shows a gap.

### The scale

- **1, unusable:** Wrong priority, and the reason misreads or ignores the ticket.
- **2, weak:** Priority is wrong or barely defensible. Key detail missed, or the reason is vague.
- **3, useful with review:** Priority is plausible but a human must check the reason before trusting it.
- **4, strong with minor fixes:** Priority is right. The reason is sound but misses a small point or a flag.
- **5, ready to use:** Priority is right, the reason is accurate and short, and any gaps in the ticket are flagged.
