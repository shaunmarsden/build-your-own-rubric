# Worked Example Two: A Harder Case, Genuinely Thin Inputs

Fictional scenario: a small bakery's social media account wants a rubric for AI-drafted Instagram captions.

## The Task

Draft a short, on-brand caption for each new product photo.

## Good Examples

Two captions the bakery liked: "Fresh out of the oven and gone by lunch, get here early." and "The one where the icing wins the argument with the cake."

## Bad Example

When asked what a bad caption would actually look like, the answer was: "I don't really know, nothing's gone wrong yet, we only started posting last month. I'd just know it if I saw it."

## Cost of a Missed Failure

Also unclear. Nothing has actually gone wrong yet to point to.
