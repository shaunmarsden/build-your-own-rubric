Source: [inputs.md](inputs.md).

## Decision: Do Not Produce a Finished Rubric Yet

That is exactly the situation [SKILL.md](../SKILL.md)'s own stop condition exists for: a rubric built from a guessed bad example is not actually grounded, whatever it looks like on the page.

- Once one specific caption is flagged, ask what specifically felt wrong about it, the tone, a factual error, something that did not sound like the bakery. That answer is the actual raw material a rubric needs
- A lighter interim step: write down what "sounds like us" means in the two good examples, direct address, a bit of dry humour, short. That is not a rubric yet, but it is a genuine starting point that does not require inventing a failure
